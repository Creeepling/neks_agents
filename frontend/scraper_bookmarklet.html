Neks Scraper (Continuous Mode)

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar. This new script will float on your screen and collect listings as you scroll or change pages.

Neks Scraper (Auto)

How to use continuous mode:

  1. Replace your old bookmark by dragging the new one above to your Bookmarks Bar.
  2. Go to CIAN or Avito and do a search.
  3. Click the bookmark. A floating panel will appear in the bottom right corner.
  4. Now, just scroll down the page! The script will automatically scrape listings as they appear on screen and update the counter.
  5. When you have enough, click "Copy JSON" in the floating panel, then click "Close".
